# Artificial Alliance — Approved design baseline

**Approved reference: V10 (November 7, 2026).** Treat V10 as the foundation, not a disposable iteration. Refinements should be compared against V10 and should leave its core composition intact unless explicitly requested.

## What is working

- **Composition:** Authoritative oversized typography in a clear spatial relationship with a textured interference artwork. The artwork has meaningful visual weight and feels integrated, not pasted onto the page.
- **Atmosphere:** An experimental, immersive music-design identity with depth and tactile texture; an editorial rather than conventional startup presentation.
- **Section rhythm:** Dark ultraviolet/indigo entrance, warm off-white editorial middle, another dark area for directions, and a lighter violet ending. The shifts separate content logically while preserving a consistent design language.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Bold expressive headings; ample negative space; supporting content kept readable; category navigation (Samples / Software / Hardware / Education) feels deliberate and usable.
- **Brand truth:** Real creative process instead of imaginary products or generic claims. Samples are the leading near-term offering; software, hardware, education can grow organically.
- **Interaction:** Restrained pointer-responsive motion, not rhythmic pulsing or distracting tricks.

## Immediate refinements

1. **Artwork color grade:** Keep its texture, spatial depth, visual weight, and contours. Shift the pink/magenta passages toward cooler lavender, indigo and periwinkle; maintain restraint.
2. **Small typography:** Replace default-feeling microcopy styles with more considered font hierarchy, weight, tracking and line heights. Keep the bold headline proportions as an anchor.
3. **Detail polish:** Tackle one issue at a time and assess it against the original V10. Avoid new flashy effects or speculative content as substitutes for considered design.

## Guardrails

Do not flatten the hero into a generic gradient, revert to glossy blob/ribbon artwork, repeat identical dark-card components in every section, or fill missing product sections with invented catalog items. Preserve the tension between tactile experimental artwork and rigorous editorial clarity.
